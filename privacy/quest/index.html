Yun's Lantern Phone app policy

Privacy — Meta Quest

Last updated 21 November 2026

Yun's Lantern for Meta Quest collects nothing. No account, no analytics, no ads. This is the whole policy.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no sign-in of its own, no name or email field, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. We never receive any information about you.

Your room, your hands and your voice

The app shows your room through the headset's passthrough and lets you play with your hands or controllers. Meta's system handles passthrough and hand tracking on the headset; the app only receives hand and controller positions while you play, uses them to move things on the table, and never stores or sends them. The app never reads camera images and never uses the microphone.

What leaves the headset

Nothing from us. When the app starts, it asks Meta's own platform whether this headset owns the app (the standard Meta Horizon Store purchase check); Meta handles that check under Meta's privacy policy. Buying the app, and the free trial, also happen entirely through Meta.

What is stored

Your progress (which lanterns you have lit) and your settings (such as Chinese-only mode) are saved on the headset and nowhere else. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Age

The app is made for learners aged ten and up. Because it collects no personal information at all, there is nothing for us to disclose, sell or share under COPPA, the GDPR, the UK Children's Code or the CCPA.

This website

This site sets no cookies, loads no fonts or scripts from other companies, and runs no analytics. It is plain files on GitHub Pages.
