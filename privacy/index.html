Privacy

Last updated 27 September 2026

Yun's Lantern collects nothing. Not a name, not an email, not an analytics event. This is the whole policy, and it is short because there is nothing to disclose.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no email field, no birthdate, no photos, no camera, no microphone, no contacts and no location. There is no advertising identifier and no device identifier.

What leaves the device

Nothing, except a purchase. The app makes no network requests at all other than to Apple's or Google's own payment service when a grown-up chooses to buy the full version. It works completely in airplane mode.

Third parties

There are none. The app contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK and no social media SDK. Nobody receives data about your child, because no data about your child exists.

What is stored

Your settings and your child's progress are saved on the device itself and nowhere else. Deleting the app deletes them. They are never uploaded.

Children

The app is made for children aged three to five and is listed in the Kids Category. Because it collects no personal information at all, it does not trigger verifiable parental consent under COPPA, and there is nothing for us to disclose, sell or share under the GDPR, the UK Children's Code or the CCPA.

This website

This site sets no cookies, loads no fonts or scripts from other companies, and runs no analytics. It is plain files on GitHub Pages.
